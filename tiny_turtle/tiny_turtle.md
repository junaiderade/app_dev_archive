# 🐢 Tiny Turtle

Journey through the ocean as a TURTLE in this EXCITING, NEW, UNDERWATER freerunner! This is more than just a game—it's an adventure filled with challenges, power-ups, and endless possibilities. You're no ordinary turtle; you're equipped with an arsenal of abilities to help you survive the deep!

Experience thrilling underwater gameplay with stunning visuals and addictive mechanics that will keep you coming back for more.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Unique Turtle Abilities** - Slow time, energy shields, rocket boosts, and more to master the ocean
- **Dynamic Obstacles** - Avoid plastic bags, plastic rings, straws, and sharks while navigating through the ocean
- **Coin Collection** - Gather coins throughout your journey to unlock new rewards
- **Character Unlocks** - Collect coins to unlock new playable turtle characters
- **Shop System** - Purchase upgrades and access new maps to expand your gameplay
- **Achievement System** - Complete a ton of achievements to challenge yourself and prove your skills
- **Game Center Integration** - Share your scores with friends and compete on leaderboards
- **Leaderboard Ranking** - Rank high and become the SHELLebrity you always wanted to be!

## 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics

- **Time Slow Power-Up** - Activate to slow down time and navigate tricky sections
- **Energy Shields** - Protect yourself from obstacles and enemies
- **Rocket Boost** - Get a speed burst to overcome challenging sections
- **Coin Collection** - Gather coins scattered throughout each level
- **Obstacle Avoidance** - Navigate through an ocean filled with hazards
